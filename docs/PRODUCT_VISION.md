# Product vision

MCQ Exam Builder reduces the fragile manual work between an educator's question bank and a ready-to-use examination package.

The product is designed around four principles: preserve answer identity, surface ambiguity instead of silently guessing, make shuffled output reproducible, and keep persistent storage out of the default workflow.

Current scope includes importing supported question-bank formats, reviewing structural findings, configuring reproducible exam versions, and exporting matching examination materials. Question generation, proctoring, grading, and student-record management are intentionally outside scope.
